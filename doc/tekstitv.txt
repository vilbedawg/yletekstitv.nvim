*tekstitv.txt*  Yle Teksti-TV in Neovim

Requires Neovim 0.10+ and `curl`. No `setup()` call is needed.

                                                                   *:Tekstitv*
:Tekstitv [page] [subpage]
        Open a page, 100 by default. Also `:Tekstitv 100.2`.
        Pages are buffers named `tekstitv://{page}/{subpage}`, so |CTRL-O|
        goes back and |:edit| reloads. Pages are cached for an hour.

                                                              *tekstitv-links*
Links are followed with "go to definition": |CTRL-]| or
|vim.lsp.buf.definition()| (often `gd`). A small built-in language server
is attached to each page for this.

                                                           *tekstitv-mappings*
Buffer-local defaults, no global mappings:

  `]p` `[p`  <Plug>(TekstitvNextPage)     <Plug>(TekstitvPrevPage)
  `]s` `[s`  <Plug>(TekstitvNextSubpage)  <Plug>(TekstitvPrevSubpage)
  `]l` `[l`  <Plug>(TekstitvNextLink)     <Plug>(TekstitvPrevLink)

Pages and subpages follow the site's own nav bar. A default is skipped if
you have mapped its <Plug> mapping yourself.

                                                            *tekstitv.setup()*
Change or disable (`false`) the default keys: >lua
    require("tekstitv").setup({
        keymaps = { next_page = "<C-n>", next_link = false },
    })
<
Keys: `next_page`, `prev_page`, `next_subpage`, `prev_subpage`, `next_link`,
`prev_link`.

                                                       *tekstitv.statusline()*
`require("tekstitv").statusline()` returns "Teksti-TV {page}.{subpage}" in
a page buffer. The numbers are also in *b:tekstitv_page* and
*b:tekstitv_subpage* .

                                                           *hl-TekstitvLink*
Links are highlighted with `TekstitvLink`, linked to |hl-Identifier|.

`:checkhealth tekstitv` checks the setup and that yle.fi is reachable.
